## Gojūon (五十音) Table

Romanization (rōmaji) for the consonant is listed in the leftmost column,
and romanization for the vowel is listed in the topmost row. Combine the
row label and column label to get the reading (e.g. row `k` + column `a`
= `ka`). Cells contain only the kana — no inline romanization — so you
have to recall the reading yourself.

|      | a   | i        | u        | e   | o        |
| ---- | --- | -------- | -------- | --- | -------- |
| –    | あ  | い       | う       | え  | お       |
| k    | か  | き       | く       | け  | こ       |
| s    | さ  | し (shi) | す       | せ  | そ       |
| t    | た  | ち (chi) | つ (tsu) | て  | と       |
| n    | な  | に       | ぬ       | ね  | の       |
| h    | は  | ひ       | ふ (fu)  | へ  | ほ       |
| m    | ま  | み       | む       | め  | も       |
| y    | や  | —        | ゆ       | —   | よ       |
| r    | ら  | り       | る       | れ  | ろ       |
| w    | わ  | —        | —        | —   | を (o)   |
| n(ん)| ん  |          |          |     |          |

## Yōon (拗音, Combined Sounds)

Small や/ゆ/よ combine with the `i`-column characters (き, し, ち, に, ひ,
み, り, ぎ, じ, び, ぴ) to form palatalized syllables:

| base | +ゃ | +ゅ | +ょ |
| ---- | --- | --- | --- |
| k    | きゃ| きゅ| きょ|
| s    | しゃ| しゅ| しょ|
| t    | ちゃ| ちゅ| ちょ|
| n    | にゃ| にゅ| にょ|
| h    | ひゃ| ひゅ| ひょ|
| m    | みゃ| みゅ| みょ|
| r    | りゃ| りゅ| りょ|

## Dakuten (濁音, Voiced Sounds)

Adding two small marks (゛, dakuten) to certain consonant rows voices the
sound:

|   | a  | i  | u  | e  | o  |
| - | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| g | が | ぎ | ぐ | げ | ご |
| z | ざ | じ | ず | ぜ | ぞ |
| d | だ | ぢ | づ | で | ど |
| b | ば | び | ぶ | べ | ぼ |

## Handakuten (半濁音, Semi-voiced Sounds)

Adding a small circle (゜, handakuten) to the `h` row gives the `p` sound:

|   | a  | i  | u  | e  | o  |
| - | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| p | ぱ | ぴ | ぷ | ぺ | ぽ |
