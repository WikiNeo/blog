## 0–10

| Numeral | Kanji | Hiragana | Romaji      |
| ------- | ----- | -------- | ----------- |
| 0       | 零    | れい/ゼロ | rei / zero  |
| 1       | 一    | いち     | ichi        |
| 2       | 二    | に       | ni          |
| 3       | 三    | さん     | san         |
| 4       | 四    | し/よん  | shi / yon   |
| 5       | 五    | ご       | go          |
| 6       | 六    | ろく     | roku        |
| 7       | 七    | しち/なな| shichi / nana|
| 8       | 八    | はち     | hachi       |
| 9       | 九    | きゅう/く| kyuu / ku   |
| 10      | 十    | じゅう   | juu         |

> ⚠️ 4, 7, and 9 have two common readings. `し` (4) and `く` (9) sound
> like the words for "death" and "suffering", so `よん` and `きゅう` are
> often preferred, especially when counting things.

## 20–90 (digit + 十)

| Numeral | Kanji | Hiragana | Romaji   |
| ------- | ----- | -------- | -------- |
| 20      | 二十  | にじゅう | ni-juu   |
| 30      | 三十  | さんじゅう| san-juu |
| 40      | 四十  | よんじゅう| yon-juu |
| 50      | 五十  | ごじゅう | go-juu   |
| 60      | 六十  | ろくじゅう| roku-juu|
| 70      | 七十  | ななじゅう| nana-juu|
| 80      | 八十  | はちじゅう| hachi-juu|
| 90      | 九十  | きゅうじゅう| kyuu-juu|

Numbers 21–99 combine these: `十 (tens) + digit`, e.g. 二十五 (ni-juu-go) = 25.

| 100     | 百    | ひゃく   | hyaku    |
| 200     | 二百  | にひゃく | ni-hyaku |
| 300     | 三百  | さんびゃく| san-byaku (sound change)|
| 400     | 四百  | よんひゃく| yon-hyaku|
| 500     | 五百  | ごひゃく | go-hyaku |
| 600     | 六百  | ろっぴゃく| rop-pyaku (sound change)|
| 700     | 七百  | ななひゃく| nana-hyaku|

> easier pronunciation: さんびゃく, ろっぴゃく, はっぴゃく.

## Ten-thousands and beyond (万)

Japanese groups large numbers by **10,000 (万, man)**, not by 1,000 like
English. This is the biggest mental shift when reading big numbers.

| Numeral     | Kanji | Hiragana  | Romaji   |
| ----------- | ----- | --------- | -------- |
| 10,000      | 一万  | いちまん  | ichi-man |
| 100,000     | 十万  | じゅうまん| juu-man  |
| 1,000,000   | 百万  | ひゃくまん| hyaku-man|
| 10,000,000  | 千万  | せんまん  | sen-man  |
| 100,000,000 | 一億  | いちおく  | ichi-oku |

## Counters (助数詞)

Japanese often requires a **counter word** attached to the number
depending on what's being counted, instead of just the bare number:

| Counter | Used for          | Example (3) | Romaji     |
| ------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| 個 (ko) | small objects      | 三個        | san-ko     |
| 人 (nin)| people             | 三人        | san-nin    |
| 本 (hon)| long thin objects  | 三本        | san-bon    |
| 枚 (mai)| flat thin objects  | 三枚        | san-mai    |
| 匹 (hiki)| small animals     | 三匹        | san-biki   |
| 歳 (sai)| age                | 三歳        | san-sai    |

> ⚠️ Some counters, people counts (1 and 2), and ages (20) have special
> irregular readings: 一人 (hitori, "one person"), 二人 (futari, "two
> people"), 二十歳 (hatachi, "20 years old").
